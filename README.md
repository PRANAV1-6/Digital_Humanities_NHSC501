FRA 2006 _Institutional_Structure_Roles_Duties_and_Rights

This network project maps the main institutions and actors represented in the Forest Rights Act 2006 process, including rights holders, the Gram Sabha and Forest Rights Committee, sub-divisional and district committees, state-level monitoring, government departments, and Panchayati Raj representatives. Directed links describe the roles and relationships recorded in the project, alongside claims, petitions, recognized rights, forest resources, and conservation responsibilities.

FRC Claim 2006 and Complaint Redressal Mechanism Map

This process network follows a claim from submission and local verification through Gram Sabha consideration, sub-divisional review, and district-level decision, then connects recognition to individual and community rights and to stewardship themes such as community forest resources, minor forest produce, management planning, and biodiversity. Appeal and monitoring routes are also represented. Together, the network projects present both an institutional view and a process view of the topic.
